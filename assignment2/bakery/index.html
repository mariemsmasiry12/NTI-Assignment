Who we are

Baking with love since 1994

We've been operating for over thirty years, sourcing organic flour from local mills and proofing every loaf by hand overnight. What started as a single family oven is now a neighbourhood staple — and every day it still makes our lives a little sweeter.

  • We care about the environment.
  • Trusted by hundreds of local families.
  • Everything baked fresh, every single day.

Our location

Find us in the heart of the city

We work out of a single, purpose-built bakehouse — big enough for a wood-fired oven, small enough that the whole street can smell it working. Stop by early for the widest choice of the day's bake.

Can't make it in? Everything on our menu is also available for local delivery — see the Contact page for details.
