Our story

Thirty years, one oven at a time

Family Bakery started as a single wood-fired oven run out of a garage, baking loaves for a handful of neighbours. Three decades on, we're still run by the same family, still mixing dough by feel, and still refusing shortcuts like additives or par-baked bases.

Every recipe on our shelf has been tasted, argued over, and refined at our own kitchen table before it ever reaches a customer — that's the only quality control we trust.
